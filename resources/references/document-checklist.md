# Shared document checklist

Editor reread and independent check both use this list.

- Confirm artifact_type, version, review_state, author, date, and reviewer slots are uniquely mapped; reviewer may be empty.
- Confirm every referenced item has a stable unique ID and literal non-overlapping boundaries.
- Confirm explicit references resolve and their dependency direction is intentional.
- Confirm diagrams have equivalent text/table coverage for permissions, amounts, conditions, and exception recovery.
- Confirm statements agree across PRD, design, clarify conclusions, plan, applicable specifications, code, and tests.
- Confirm unknown facts remain explicit open questions and no edit is presented as approval.
- Confirm source versions/fingerprints and baseline; a stale editor result returns for merge or redispatch rather than overwriting.
